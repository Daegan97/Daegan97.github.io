Pet Loss Support Hotline

“Your world stopped turning.”

Long-form print spread, spec work · 1 min read

A long-form print spread I wrote on spec for a university pet-loss support hotline: slow, specific and quiet, for a reader who is grieving.

Spec work: unsolicited and unpaid. Not commissioned by, affiliated with or endorsed by any institution. Names and contact details removed.

The copy

Your world stopped turning.
You don’t have to apologize for that here.

The rest of the world expects you to be over it by Monday.

They do not see the quiet moments when the grief catches you.

It is the pause when you unlock the front door.

It is the habit of listening for a sound that is no longer there, or stepping around a familiar spot in the hallway.

It is the heavy stillness that settles over the house in the late afternoon.

When you lose the center of your world, everyday routines become difficult to navigate.

You find yourself moving through rooms that suddenly feel entirely too large.

It is exhausting to justify this depth of heartbreak to people who insist it was just an animal.

They do not understand that this loss touches every single part of your day.

At our Pet Loss Support Hotline, we do not measure grief by species.

Our hotline is staffed by volunteer veterinary students, trained alongside professional grief counselors.

This is a safe place where your heartache needs no disclaimer. No one here will ask you to hurry up and heal.

It’s exactly as heavy as it feels.

Let us help you carry it for a minute.

Talk to someone who speaks your language.

You don’t even have to give your name.
